Title: Concurrency, rate limits, and backoff
Tags: rate limits, concurrency, quota, backoff, 429
Updated: 2026-02-27

Limits are expressed as concurrent calls plus requests per minute on the
management API. Starter allows 5 concurrent calls, Growth 25, and Enterprise is
sized per contract. Each tier also carries a 600 requests-per-minute ceiling on
REST calls made with the same API key.

Breaching an API limit returns HTTP 429 with a Retry-After header. The header
value is authoritative: clients should sleep exactly that long and add jitter
before retrying rather than retrying immediately, because a synchronized retry
storm from a fleet of workers is the usual cause of a second 429.

A concurrent-call breach does not return an error. The new inbound call is
routed to the overflow trunk or, if none is configured, terminated with a
busy tone after two rings. Outbound campaigns are paced client-side; the
platform will queue rather than reject, but the queue drains in order and a
campaign that outruns capacity simply starts late.

Limit increases are self-service on Growth and above, effective immediately, and
are billed on the next invoice from the effective date.
