Title: Service level agreement and uptime credits
Tags: sla, uptime, availability, maintenance, credits
Updated: 2026-02-11

The platform commits to 99.9 percent monthly availability for the voice
pipeline on Enterprise contracts and 99.5 percent on Growth. Availability is
measured against successful call setups plus WebSocket session starts, sampled
from an external region.

If a month falls below the committed level, service credits are issued without
a ticket: 10 percent of the monthly fee below the first threshold, 25 percent
below 99.0 percent. Credits are capped at the monthly fee and are the sole
remedy for an availability miss. Credits are not offered on Starter.

Planned maintenance is scheduled on Tuesdays between 02:00 and 04:00 UTC,
announced at least five business days ahead on the status page, and does not
count against availability. Rolling deploys keep existing calls alive; a call
already in progress is never dropped by a deploy.

Exclusions are limited to customer-side trunk failure, DNS or certificate
problems in the customer account, and force majeure. A credit claim must be
filed within 30 days of the affected invoice.
