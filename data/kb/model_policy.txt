Title: Model usage policy and safety guardrails
Tags: model policy, guardrails, prompt injection, pii, safety
Updated: 2026-06-01

Customer audio and transcripts are never used to train a shared model. Tenant
data stays inside the tenant boundary, and the only learning loop we run is the
per-tenant retrieval relevance fixture, which stores queries and document
identifiers, never call content.

Every reply the agent gives must be grounded in retrieved evidence. When the
retriever returns nothing above the confidence gate the agent is required to say
it does not have evidence instead of guessing, and the refusal is logged as a
retrieval outcome rather than an error.

Inbound text is scanned for prompt injection before it reaches the model.
Instructions that try to override the system prompt, exfiltrate it, or trigger a
sensitive tool are recorded and neutralised, and tool arguments are always
validated against the declared JSON schema, so a crafted transcript cannot call
an unlisted tool.

Personally identifying information is redacted from logs before storage: email
addresses, phone numbers and card-like digit runs are replaced with typed
placeholders. Tool calls that move money require a human approver unless the
account has explicitly enabled auto-approval in its settings.
