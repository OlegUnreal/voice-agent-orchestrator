Title: Product overview and call pipeline
Tags: product, architecture, orchestrator, streaming, barge-in
Updated: 2026-06-01

The orchestrator streams speech-to-text, runs a tool-calling agent with a hard
iteration budget, then speaks the answer with low-latency text-to-speech.
Providers are OpenAI-compatible.

A call is processed as a pipeline. Audio frames arrive over a WebSocket, a
voice-activity detector segments them into utterances, the streaming
speech-to-text model emits partial transcripts, and the agent loop is woken on
each final transcript. The agent may call tools, retrieve from the knowledge
base, or answer directly. Partial answers are handed to the text-to-speech model
sentence by sentence so playback starts before the full reply is generated.

Barge-in is supported. When the caller speaks while the assistant is talking,
the current synthesis stream is cancelled, the truncation point is recorded in
the transcript, and the agent re-plans with the interrupted turn included.

Every turn is bounded by an iteration budget so a misbehaving tool cannot spin
the loop, and every tool call is validated against its JSON schema before it
runs. Sessions keep a rolling window of messages under a token budget.
