Title: Security, certifications, and encryption
Tags: security, soc2, encryption, iso27001, penetration test
Updated: 2026-01-28

Northwind Voice Labs holds SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001. The current SOC 2 audit
report and the most recent penetration test summary are shared under NDA;
send a request to the security mailbox from an authorised address and the
report is delivered within two business days.

Audio in transit is carried over TLS 1.2 or higher for API traffic and SRTP for
media. Audio at rest is encrypted with AES-256 using per-tenant keys, and
transcript text is encrypted in a separate store so a leaked transcript cannot
be joined to raw media without both keys.

Access to production is gated by single sign-on with hardware keys, just-in-time
elevations that expire in four hours, and an immutable audit log. Customer audio
is not accessible from the general engineering role; only the on-call
telephony engineer holds a break-glass path, and every use is reviewed weekly.

Subprocessors are listed in the trust centre and customers receive 30 days of
notice before a new subprocessor that touches customer content is added.
