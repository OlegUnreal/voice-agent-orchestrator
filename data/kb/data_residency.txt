Title: Data residency and regional processing
Tags: data residency, regions, eu, gdpr, transfer
Updated: 2026-04-22

Workloads run in three regions: eu-central, us-east, and ap-southeast. A tenant
is pinned to one region at contract signing and all of its media, transcripts,
embeddings and retrieval indexes stay in that region. There is no cross-region
replication of customer audio.

For EU tenants, model inference for speech-to-text and text-to-speech is served
from the in-region OpenAI-compatible endpoint. When a provider cannot serve a
region, that fact is disclosed in the data processing agreement and the affected
capability is disabled rather than silently routed elsewhere.

Metadata kept outside the region is limited to aggregate counters used for
billing and capacity planning: call duration, byte totals, error rates. Those
counters carry no audio, no transcript text, and no caller number.

A tenant that changes region requires a migration window because indexes must
be rebuilt in the destination. Requests are scheduled monthly and the old
region is wiped and certified 14 days after cutover.
