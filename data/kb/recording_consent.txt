Title: Call recording and consent capture
Tags: recording, consent, retention, disclosure
Updated: 2026-05-06

Recording is off by default and enabled per trunk. When enabled, the caller
hears a disclosure line before the agent answers, and the accepted consent is
written to the call record with the timestamp and the disclosure version that
was played.

Consent handling differs by jurisdiction. Two-party regions require the caller
to say a confirmation phrase, which the pipeline captures as an explicit
transcript segment; one-party regions rely on the disclosure tone. The
jurisdiction is derived from the trunk country, never from the caller location,
and the mapping is reviewed quarterly.

Recordings and their transcripts are retained for 90 days by default. Growth
tenants may select 30 or 90 days and Enterprise tenants may select up to 365
days. After the retention window the media object is deleted first and the
transcript text is purged in the nightly sweep, so a call older than the window
cannot be re-listened to even by an administrator.

A caller may ask to be forgotten mid-call. The agent ends the recording flag,
and the call is scheduled for deletion the same day rather than at the end of
the retention period.
