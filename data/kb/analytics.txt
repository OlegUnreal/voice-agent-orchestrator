Title: Analytics, dashboards, and metric definitions
Tags: analytics, dashboard, metrics, containment rate, reporting
Updated: 2026-04-08

The console reports volume, quality and cost in one place, with every metric
defined in the tooltip so two teams cannot disagree about what containment
means. Definitions are versioned; if a metric changes shape the changelog says
so and the old series is kept alongside.

Containment rate counts calls resolved without a human, and excludes calls
where the caller asked for a person within the first two turns. Comprehension
failure rate counts consecutive turns where the transcript confidence fell
below the threshold. Abandonment is measured against hold, not against the
whole call, because a caller who hangs up during a transfer is not an
abandoned bot call.

Dashboards are per trunk and per intent, refresh within five minutes, and cover
the current day plus any retained history window the tenant selected. Exports
are CSV from the UI or newline-delimited JSON from the API, and a scheduled
report can be emailed weekly to a distribution list.

Custom dimensions let you tag a call with up to ten key-value pairs from a tool
result and slice every metric by them, which is how most teams end up tracking
outcomes per campaign rather than per number.
