Title: Refund policy for prepaid minutes
Tags: refunds, billing, prepaid minutes, policy
Updated: 2026-05-12

Northwind Voice Labs refunds unused prepaid minutes within 14 days of purchase.
Refunds after 14 days require manager approval. Partial months are not prorated.
To request one, open a billing ticket with the invoice number and the minute
balance you believe is unused; the balance snapshot taken at the time of the
request is the one used for the calculation.

Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days
of approval. Card refunds appear as a credit from Northwind Voice Labs. Bank
transfer credits can take up to 15 business days depending on the receiving
bank. We do not refund to third-party accounts or to a different payment method
than the one that was charged.

Promotional credits, trial minutes, and volume discounts that were never
invoiced are not refundable because no payment was taken for them. If a usage
spike was caused by a documented platform incident, the affected minutes are
credited on the next invoice rather than refunded, and the credit appears as a
separate line item called incident credit.
