Title: Transfer to a human agent
Tags: escalation, transfer, human, handoff, queue, warm transfer
Updated: 2026-05-22

The agent transfers to a human when the caller asks for one, when the topic is
out of scope, when two consecutive turns fail comprehension, or when a tool the
caller needs is unavailable. Transfer is never held back to force a resolution.

Warm transfer passes context: the receiving agent sees the transcript so far,
the intent the classifier settled on, the tool results, and the caller waiting
time before the transfer. A cold transfer only passes the phone number and is
available on Starter.

Queue behaviour depends on your contact centre. If a seat is free the call is
bridged with hold audio; if nobody answers within the timeout the caller is
offered a callback slot or a ticket, and the choice is logged. Callbacks are
scheduled in the caller's own timezone even when the queue is measured in UTC.

Out-of-hours handling is a per-trunk setting and follows the support window for
the tenant: the caller is told when a human will next be reachable rather than
being told the office is closed. Transfers to an external number use the
secondary trunk if the first fails.
