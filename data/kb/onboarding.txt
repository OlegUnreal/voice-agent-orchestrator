Title: Onboarding, sandbox, and go-live checklist
Tags: onboarding, sandbox, migration, rollout, go-live
Updated: 2026-05-30

Onboarding runs in four weeks by default. Week one is the sandbox: you get an
API key, a test trunk, and a synthetic caller harness that replays recorded
conversations so flows can be tested without dialling real numbers. Sandbox
traffic is free within the 500 minute allowance.

Week two is knowledge. Upload your policy documents, product facts and support
answers, then review the retrieval trace for your own questions so you can see
which chunk the agent cited. Documents that produce empty retrieval results are
the single most common cause of a bad launch, and the console flags them.

Week three is a shadow pilot: a small percentage of live traffic, recordings
sampled for human review, no agent writes to your systems. Week four ramps by
area code. The go-live checklist covers caller ID attestation, the overflow
trunk, the webhook endpoint and its signature verification, consent disclosure
for each region, and an escalation path to a human queue.

Migration from another vendor is usually a port of numbers plus a re-record of
the greeting; call flows are not portable between platforms.
