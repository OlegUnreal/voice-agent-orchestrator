Title: Incident management and postmortems
Tags: incidents, status page, postmortem, severity, rca
Updated: 2026-03-25

Severity definitions are fixed: Sev-1 is total loss of voice traffic or a
data exposure, Sev-2 is a degraded region or sustained quality loss, Sev-3 is a
single-tenant or cosmetic fault. A Sev-1 opens a bridge within 5 minutes of
detection and a page is sent to the on-call engineer immediately.

Customers see an incident on the public status page within 15 minutes of the
page going out, with updates at least every 30 minutes while it is active.
Affected tenants also receive a targeted notification through the webhook and
the console banner.

Every Sev-1 and Sev-2 gets a written postmortem published within five business
days. Postmortems are blameless, name a trigger, a contributing set of causes,
and time-to-detect, time-to-mitigate and time-to-resolve. Each remediation item
has an owner and a due date, and the follow-through is reviewed in the weekly
operations meeting rather than left to the author.

We do not publish a postmortem for a Sev-3 unless a customer was billed
incorrectly.
