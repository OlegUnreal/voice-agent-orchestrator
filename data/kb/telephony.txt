Title: Telephony, SIP trunking, and numbers
Tags: telephony, sip, trunk, codec, caller id
Updated: 2026-03-02

Inbound calls arrive over SIP from your carrier or from numbers we provision.
We support G.711 ulaw and alaw, and Opus at 48 kilobits per second when the
trunk offers it; the media codec is negotiated per call and reported in the call
detail record.

Trunks are registered by SBC address or by an IP allow list, and both use TLS
for signalling. Failover is ordered: if the primary trunk does not answer a
new INVITE within 2 seconds, the call is retried on the secondary trunk before
any error tone is played, so a single-carrier outage is invisible to callers.

Outbound caller ID must be provisioned per number with the attestation level
your carrier signs. We can present a pool of local numbers by area code and
will rotate within the pool for campaigns, keeping the same number for a
return call so callbacks land in the same queue.

Call detail records are pushed to your webhook endpoint within 30 seconds of
teardown and include duration, disposition, codec, trunk, and the first and
last digit strings captured during the call.
