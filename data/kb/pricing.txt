Title: Pricing tiers and per-minute rates
Tags: pricing, tiers, rates, volume discount
Updated: 2026-05-20

Pricing is published in three tiers. Starter covers a single trunk and up to 5
concurrent calls at the standard per-minute rate. Growth raises concurrency to
25 and includes the knowledge-base retrieval tooling and call analytics.
Enterprise adds dedicated capacity, custom retention, and a contractual uptime
commitment.

Per-minute rates are billed by the second with a one-minute minimum and are
quoted inbound and outbound separately. Rates drop in bands: usage above 10,000
minutes per month is discounted 8 percent, above 50,000 minutes 15 percent, and
above 200,000 minutes is negotiated. Discounts are applied to the invoice as a
separate line, never baked into the published rate.

Prepaid minute bundles are sold in blocks of 1,000 minutes and expire 12 months
after purchase. Expired bundles are not renewed automatically and cannot be
restored at the original rate.

Development and sandbox traffic is free up to 500 minutes per month so a
team can load test without a bill; sandbox minutes never count toward a volume
band.
