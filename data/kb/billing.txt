Title: Invoices, payment methods, and dunning
Tags: billing, invoice, payment, dunning, tax
Updated: 2026-03-18

Invoices are issued on the first business day of each month for the prior
usage period and are emailed to the billing contact on file. A PDF and a
CSV breakdown of per-minute usage by trunk are available in the console.

We accept card, SEPA direct debit, and bank transfer in EUR, USD, and GBP.
Card and direct debit are charged automatically three days after the invoice
date. Bank transfer accounts carry your reference number; payments without it
land in an unmatched queue and delay reconciliation.

Dunning runs on a fixed schedule. A failed charge is retried at day 1, day 3,
and day 7. Concurrency is capped at the contract limit during a billing
dispute only if the dispute is raised before the invoice due date; otherwise
service continues and the disputed line is credited later.

Value added tax is applied at the rate of the billing country. Reverse charge
applies to EU business customers who store a valid VAT identification number
before the first invoice is generated.
