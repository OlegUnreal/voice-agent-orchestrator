Title: Support hours and paging
Tags: support, hours, paging, escalation
Updated: 2026-04-02

Northwind Voice Labs support is available Monday to Friday 09:00-18:00 UTC.
Severity-1 voice outages are paged 24/7.

Standard tickets are answered in the order they are received during support
hours. The first response target is 4 business hours for Severity 2 and 1
business day for Severity 3. Messages sent after 18:00 UTC are queued and picked
up the next business morning in UTC order, not in the sender's local time.

Severity 1 means production voice traffic cannot be placed or answered, or more
than 20 percent of calls fail. Sev-1 pages route to the on-call telephony
engineer through the paging service and escalate to the engineering manager
after 15 minutes without an acknowledgement. Paging is available every day,
including weekends and public holidays, for Sev-1 only.

Non-urgent questions about invoices, contract changes, or rollout planning are
handled by the customer success team in the same window and are never paged.
