Title: Supported languages and accents
Tags: languages, locales, accents, translation, coverage
Updated: 2026-04-30

Speech understanding ships for 24 languages with telephone-tuned acoustic
models, and synthesis for 18 of them. English, German, French, Spanish,
Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Swedish, Norwegian, Danish, Turkish,
Japanese, Korean, Mandarin and Hindi cover the deployed base; the console lists
the current matrix per capability, because understanding and synthesis coverage
are not the same set.

Language is detected from the first 800 milliseconds of an utterance and can
switch mid-call. A caller who moves between languages gets the reply in the
language they just used, and the transcript keeps the original language plus a
normalised English gloss for the CRM record.

Accent robustness is measured separately from language coverage. Broad regional
accents are supported in every listed language; heavy first-language interference
raises word error rate noticeably in Japanese and Mandarin, and those queues are
the recommended candidates for a human fallback.

Custom vocabularies are per language, so a German deployment should not register
English product codes in the same biasing list.
