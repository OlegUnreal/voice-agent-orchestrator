Title: Cancellation, export, and data deletion
Tags: cancellation, deletion, export, retention, contract
Updated: 2026-01-15

Cancellation requires 30 days written notice on annual contracts and is
effective at the end of the current billing period, never mid-period. Monthly
plans can be cancelled from the console and stop at the next renewal date.
Prepaid minutes that remain unused at termination are handled under the refund
policy window that applied when they were bought.

Export is self-service and unlimited. The console produces newline-delimited
JSON for transcripts, a CSV for call detail records, and signed links for raw
media that stay valid for 24 hours. Exports include the retrieval index
contents so a customer can rebuild search elsewhere, and they can be triggered
from the API with a manage-scoped key.

Deletion runs on a schedule: the tenant is disabled immediately, media is
destroyed within 7 days, and derived data such as embeddings, indexes and
aggregated analytics within 30 days. Backups roll off over 35 days, and the
final certificate of deletion names the backup expiry date rather than claiming
an instant wipe.

Support tickets are retained for the statutory period of the billing country
after cancellation and are deleted with the tenant record afterwards.
